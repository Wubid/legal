Wubid Privacy Policy

Wubid is a personal sushi tasting journal for iOS. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, how it is stored, and the choices available to you.

Who we are

Wubid helps you record tasting sessions, bite scores, photos, optional voice impressions, and private album exports. The app is designed to work primarily on your device and does not require creating an online account to use core tasting features.

Information stored on your device

  • Session details such as shop name, visit date, seat type, and status
  • Bite entries including neta name, type, scores, sauce notes, Love It / Pin flags, and text impressions
  • Photos you capture with the camera or select from your photo library for a bite or album
  • Optional voice notes converted to text when you enable speech recognition
  • Usual sets, album exports, credit balances, and local purchase records
  • Lightweight preferences such as onboarding completion

This information remains in on-device storage (for example UserDefaults and the app Documents directory) unless you choose to export or save images to Photos.

Permissions

  • Camera — requested only when you tap Capture Plate to photograph a piece.
  • Photo Library — requested only when you choose an existing photo or save an album image.
  • Microphone — requested only when you start a voice note.
  • Speech Recognition — requested only when converting a voice note into text.
  • Notifications — a system prompt may appear once when you first reach the home screen. This A-side build does not schedule marketing or tasting push messages after that prompt, and it does not set a notification center delegate that would conflict with other modules.

Wubid does not request App Tracking Transparency and does not use the advertising identifier for tracking across apps or websites.

Purchases

Consumable gift packs are processed by Apple In-App Purchase. Wubid stores only the local credit balance and purchase identifiers needed for idempotent bookkeeping on this device. There is no restore-purchases flow for these consumable credits. Credits stay on the current installation and are not recoverable after deleting the app or clearing all data.

Network and analytics

Opening Privacy Policy or Terms of Use loads the published pages in an in-app web view. Purchase validation uses Apple frameworks. Wubid does not operate a tasting-data backend that uploads your sessions for ranking or recommendation.

Data retention and deletion

You can remove local content with Clear All Data, which deletes sessions, media drafts, credits, and preferences stored by the app on this device. Log Out returns you to the welcome screen without deleting tasting records. Uninstalling the app also removes its sandbox data.

Children

Wubid is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes

We may revise this Privacy Policy to reflect product or legal changes. Continued use of Wubid after a revision means you accept the updated policy as presented in the app.

Contact

For privacy questions about Wubid, use App Store product support associated with the Wubid listing.
